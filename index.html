Calculated Attrition Count, Attrition Rate, Average Attrition Age, and salary comparisons between active and departed staff.

"What actually drives attrition?"

KEY FINDINGS
  • Competitors offering better deals or devices were identified as the leading churn driver.
  • Customers with a higher number of service calls showed significantly higher churn.
  • Month-to-month contracts had a markedly higher churn rate than one- and two-year contracts.
